# Human Review Protocol

[中文](../Human_Review.md) | **English**

> AI output ≠ approved knowledge.

Model output is candidate knowledge by default. It may become a durable final
`Course_Knowledge.md` only after human review and approval.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Course scope, objectives, and section structure match the complete Evidence
- [ ] Numbers
- [ ] Percentages
- [ ] Units
- [ ] Dates and times
- [ ] Personal names
- [ ] Companies and brands
- [ ] Standard identifiers
- [ ] Product models
- [ ] Technical terminology
- [ ] Critical causal relationships
- [ ] Conditions, limitations, exceptions, and failure cases
- [ ] Whether the AI added content absent from the source
- [ ] Whether instructor views, learner speculation, and model inference are
      distinguished correctly
- [ ] Whether every `[TO CONFIRM]` marker was resolved or deliberately retained
- [ ] Whether source identifiers exist and timestamps point to the claimed evidence
- [ ] Whether cross-section merging changed the original meaning
- [ ] Whether deduplication removed any new information
- [ ] Whether the Markdown document is complete and independently readable

## Approval record

The final document should record its review status, reviewer or reviewer role,
review date, and any remaining limitations. Without an approval record, its
status remains “candidate.”
